# Fondos del laboratorio

Generados con image_gen integrado, sin CLI, el 8 de octubre de 2026. Se optimizan a WebP (calidad 83). Son ilustraciones ambientales inspiradas en el laboratorio de Tony Stark; las fotografías de aficiones y los logos reales se mantienen independientes. Fondos al 24% de opacidad con degradado azul oscuro.

## control

Archivo: public/images/laboratory/control.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background image for an Iron Man inspired developer portfolio section. Create a single cinematic architectural rendering of a high-tech control room with a central circular reactor console and large metal-framed windows. It belongs to one coherent understated engineering laboratory. Materials: brushed metal, matte white walls, metallic navy blue and deep metallic red panels. ONLY white, red and blue hues. Realistic restrained architecture, subtle believable reflections, soft white lighting, clean and elegant, no people, no characters, no logos, no readable text, no floating holograms. Wide landscape 16:9 composition with room for website content. Designed to be viewed behind a dark navy translucent overlay, keep distinct architectural forms and generous negative space, no excessive clutter. This is fictional environmental concept art, not a personal photograph.
